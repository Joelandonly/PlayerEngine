ⓘ

In Minecraft: Type /ellie followed by a space, then use Tab or the arrow keys to choose a command and its arguments. For example, try /ellie ellie_storage status or /ellie get cobblestone 64. Slash commands go to the nearest loaded companion you own in your current dimension. You can also send ellie get cobblestone 64 in the chat Ellie receives. Both forms use the native command route. <...> = required argument, [...] = optional.

netherbridge

Build & setup

Build a straight, three-wide Nether highway with three blocks of headroom and a netherrack floor.

Syntax
/ellie netherbridge <north|south|east|west> <1–256> [originX originY originZ]
Examples 2
/ellie netherbridge east 32
/ellie netherbridge east 32 100 70 200
Start on solid ground in the Nether. The first row is one block ahead of the origin, at its walking height. Ellie gathers netherrack as needed. Use the same origin to continue verified work after a stop. Ellie bridges over lava, fills lava at floor height, and seals reachable lava that would enter the walking space. Safe Zones, portals, and containers can block a row.

hero

Combat & defense

Guard your position with twelve animated combat techniques. Ellie picks a move based on enemy distance, crowds, and her health.

Syntax
/ellie hero [on|off|status]
Examples 3
/ellie hero
/ellie hero status
/ellie hero off
Moves Crimson Gaze weakens one foe. Crow Murder hits clusters. Black Flame erupts around one target. Phantom Blade sweeps and knocks back. Moon Prison slows a group. Spirit Armor briefly grants resistance and pushes nearby threats back. Scarlet Lance pierces lined-up mobs. Mirror Step strikes with two shadow echoes and weakens its target. Gravity Well pulls a group together. Thunder Chain jumps between up to four visible enemies. Comet Fall hits a distant target from above. Crimson Fan spreads a volley across a cone.
Guard range Ellie notices hostiles up to 32 blocks horizontally and 3 blocks vertically from her owner. She can cast on visible threats within 14 blocks of her. The guard lasts until turned off or replaced by another task. Each mob kill credited to Ellie grants companion XP based on the mob type.

ellie_storage

Storage & GPS

Build and manage a warehouse of double chests for automatic inventory recovery.

Syntax
ellie_storage [init here | resume | expand [count] | status | list | organize | off | on | reserve <free slots> | register <x> <y> <z> | approve_floor]
Examples 12
ellie ellie_storage status
ellie ellie_storage init here
ellie ellie_storage resume
ellie ellie_storage expand 4
ellie ellie_storage list
ellie ellie_storage organize
ellie ellie_storage off
ellie ellie_storage on
ellie ellie_storage reserve 4
ellie ellie_storage register 101 65 101
ellie ellie_storage approve_floor
ellie ellie_storage expand 8
Candidate Commands use the warehouse in Ellie’s current dimension. A new Nether warehouse has a roof and a bent entrance. Ellie can mix blackstone and nether brick blocks. The bare command shows status. Init checks the site at Ellie’s feet before saving it. Ellie prepares tools, clears survival-breakable blocks, including player-placed blocks, outside Safe Zones, and builds dry support columns up to 12 blocks tall from solid ground. You can choose a different site until site work or chest registration starts. Mutations require an authenticated owner. Expansion adds four double chests by default. A count such as 8 adds two modules, up to 32 double chests total. Register applies only to a chest in an exact planned pair. Approve floor applies to existing floor material in the planned footprint. Ellie estimates how many new slots the active task needs, using room in partial stacks. Reserve sets the extra room she tries to free per storage trip, up to her main inventory capacity. Cleanup can store ordinary surplus and superseded, unequipped tools while keeping active recipe and project supplies. A warehouse was built and expanded to 12 double chests in-game. Mining and crafting also resumed after warehouse deposits.

Which build includes these commands?

The warehouse candidate for Minecraft 1.21.1 Fabric includes both waterfill and ellie_storage. The Windows build and warehouse fixture checks passed. Warehouse construction, expansion and inventory recovery were exercised in-game.

How Safe Zones and waterfill interact

Safe Zones forbid Ellie’s terrain editing. Waterfill must skip protected sources, including entire protected building areas. Existing blocks outside that area are never supposed to be replaced. The region is centered on the authenticated owner, using the same horizontal and vertical radius convention as safezone here.
